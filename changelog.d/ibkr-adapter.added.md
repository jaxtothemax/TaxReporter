- **Interactive Brokers**: TaxReporter now reads Interactive Brokers Activity Flex Query statements (XML), every account in the file, including the old and new accounts of a client moved between IB entities. It reads executed trades at their contract price and exchange date, dividends with the tax withheld on them, and stock splits, and it checks a split's ratio against the shares IBKR says it added. Lots are matched first in, first out across Interactive Brokers and Trading 212 together. Summary rows are never counted twice, a reversed dividend is left out with its tax, and option trades and interest are set aside with a note, as the returns they belong on are not built yet. What it cannot read safely, such as a cancelled or corrected trade, a payment in lieu of a dividend, a merger or a spin-off, stops the return with its own finding instead of being guessed. The file is read as strict XML, so nothing in it can make the reader fetch or expand anything.
