- **Dividends from your broker exports**: TaxReporter can now turn dividends and the tax withheld on them into the Doh-Div return, one record per payment as FURS requires. Each payment and its withheld tax are converted at the Banka Slovenije rate of the payment day, and a later refund of withheld tax is netted against the tax it refunds. Fund distributions get their own type code. When one payer pays twice on one day, the records are numbered the way FURS's guide advises, so eDavki does not reject them. The estimate credits foreign tax only up to the treaty rate (15% for US dividends) and the Slovenian 25%, and says how much was withheld above the treaty rate. The payer's address has to be supplied, because brokers do not export it. The app and the CLI do not use it yet.
