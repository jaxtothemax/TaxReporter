- **A Trading 212 takeover or free rights hold back only the returns they can change**: a Trading 212 takeover paid in shares, or rights handed out free, used to withhold Doh-KDVP and Doh-Div for every year. Now they withhold Doh-KDVP and Doh-Div only for the years they can change, and appear as a note in the other years: a 2025 takeover no longer stops the 2026 returns when neither security was sold in 2026. No figure on a written return changes. The command line now exits with 0 when only such notes remain (ADR 0017).
