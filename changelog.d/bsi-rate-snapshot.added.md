- **Banka Slovenije exchange rates**: TaxReporter now carries every Banka Slovenije rate since 2007, daily and monthly, exactly as published, and picks the one the law asks for: the list of the day, or the last one before it on a weekend or holiday (never more than 10 days back), the monthly list for currencies like TWD, and the fixed euro rate after a changeover such as Bulgaria's. Pence quotes are converted at the pound's rate, and the one day in 2025 where Banka Slovenije's NOK rate differs from the ECB's uses Banka Slovenije's value with a note.
