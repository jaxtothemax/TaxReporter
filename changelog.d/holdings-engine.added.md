- **Holdings in the command line's JSON report**: `--json` now lists the shares still held, in two views. One is each account's own position, which you can check against what the broker shows. The other is the FIFO lots still open across all your accounts, the ones your next sale will be taxed against. Each lot shows its purchase date, its cost in euro at the Banka Slovenije rate of that day, and the date it moves to a lower tax rate. That date is the day after the anniversary, because FURS has not said whether the anniversary itself counts. Accounts appear as "ibkr-1" and never by account number. Holdings are as of the last day your files cover, never block a return, and are flagged where shares were moved between accounts or a row was refused.
