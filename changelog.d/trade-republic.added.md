- **Trade Republic**: TaxReporter now reads Trade Republic's transaction export (CSV), in the app and on the command line. Purchases, savings plans and sales of shares and funds go on Doh-KDVP at their own price, each on its Slovenian date, and a fund is marked as one because the export says so. Overlapping exports are read once. Deposits, withdrawals and card payments are set aside, and interest is flagged, as it belongs on a return TaxReporter does not build yet. Dividends are not read yet: what the export's amounts mean on a dividend is not confirmed, so a file with one stops both returns with a finding that says so, rather than filing an amount that may be converted at Trade Republic's own rate. Free shares (Saveback, stock perks), trades with a foreign currency leg, corporate actions, bonds and crypto stop the return with their own reason too.
