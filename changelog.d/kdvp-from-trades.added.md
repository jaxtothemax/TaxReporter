- **Capital gains from your trades**: TaxReporter can now turn the trades of every broker into the Doh-KDVP inventory lists. It matches each sale to the oldest shares first, per security across all brokers, and carries purchases through stock splits with their original dates. Each purchase and sale is converted at the Banka Slovenije rate of its own trade date, and shares held 15 years or more are left off, as the law exempts them. Column 10 follows the 30-day rule: a loss counts unless the same security is bought within 30 days before or after the sale, and then only the replaced part is excluded. While your files end inside those 30 days, the column stays empty and a warning says so. No return is produced while a purchase or a rate is missing, and the tax estimate is worked out the way eDavki will compute it. The app and the CLI do not use it yet.
