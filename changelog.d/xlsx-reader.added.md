- **Excel workbooks (XLSX), read safely**: the command line can now open the XLSX files that eToro, XTB and Saxo export, with TaxReporter's own strict reader and no outside library. It reads only what a broker's workbook holds: the list of sheets, and the cells of a sheet when one is needed, each number read from its exact text to the 15 significant digits a spreadsheet keeps, and each date in the workbook's own date system. A file that is not laid out as Excel lays one out, that carries macros, or that would unpack to more than 64 MiB is refused with its reason; once a broker's sheets are read, a formula or a malformed cell is refused with the sheet and cell where reading stopped. No broker's workbook is recognized yet: eToro's comes first, and until it does an XLSX export is opened, checked and reported as not recognized, and the web app does not take XLSX files.
