- **Doh-KDVP and Doh-Div XML**: TaxReporter can now write both returns in the exact shape eDavki imports, element order included, and every sample file is checked against FURS's own schemas. Before writing, it checks what those schemas let through but eDavki refuses: an 8-digit tax number, a purchase and a sale in every list, no running stock below zero, positive dividend amounts with foreign tax no higher than the gross, FURS's country codes, and one record per payer and day. Amounts use exact decimal arithmetic and are rounded only at the form field. The app and the CLI do not use the writers yet.
